# Design review, 2026-10-29

_Against [design brief v1.0](../plan/11-desktop-design-brief.md). Sean asked for it on 2026-10-29. Every screen was captured by the harness in `design/capture/`: 239 states across 73 screens (desktop 181, web vault 25, extension 33), in light and dark, at 1280, 900 and 390 px, with an axe scan of each state. The states can be tapped through at https://claude.ai/artifact/RpyQgTKGmTEpwSbfAbVmcW; each link below opens one. Findings carry permanent ids (R1, R2…); the fix list at the end orders them into builds._

## The answer

The app is coherent at the component level. unicron carries the type, spacing and controls, the security components behave, and the wording is plain almost everywhere. What hasn't kept up is the composition. Slices 13–29 each added their panel to a screen that was designed for fewer, so the item detail, the editor, Security and Sharing are now long, flat stacks where everything has the same weight. At phone width the same screens break in ways that matter: approve and deny lose their words. Seven findings are defects to fix now (R1–R7); eight are structural changes that are Sean's design call (R8–R15, each with a recommendation); the rest is polish.

## What works

- **Plain words.** Findings, gates, rights and tiers read as sentences, not jargon ("Someone could already be using these.", "Fill freely", "Fills in the browser only; never shown or copied"). Brief rule 5 (words, never colour or an icon alone) holds on desktop widths.
- **The lock model.** Unlock, the locked wash and first run are calm and clear, with one primary action each.
- **The codes view.** Large tabular digits, issuer and account above, copy at the right edge; the HOTP counter and "Next code" read well.
- **Status proofs.** One line per proof with a "verified" label and "Check now", as decided in D95.
- **Unknown-state honesty.** The quantum-safe line, Security's facts and the restore-drill sentence all say "can't check" rather than guess.

## What this review couldn't see

The server's own pages (admin, email approval, operator kit), OS and browser surfaces Lockdown doesn't draw, the live camera, real TOTP timing and zxcvbn scores (the sample data stands in), and in-page frame states one trusted click deep, which the review build lets you tap. The countdown rings in the codes view draw empty in the captures because the sample clock is frozen; check them in the app. Full list: `design/capture/README.md`.
