| D10 | 2026-10-15 | **Any app can create the identity; the first one installed owns it until Lockdown adopts it.** Each app can create a login, identity and persona on its own, with no other app present. | D11 | 2026-10-15 | **The recovery kit is a property of the identity, not of the app that made it.** Whichever app first creates the recovery kit (Secret Key + recovery code) creates it for the shared identity. Consequence: every app-level data key must be wrapped to the identity key from the start, or that data stays out of reach of the kit. | Sean |

| D53 | 2026-10-17 | **Head acceptance, as implemented.** `PUT /v1/vaults/{id}/heads` takes a head when: the vault is the account's; the signer is a device of the account (D51) and the signature verifies; `seq` is exactly the server's current `seq` + 1 (`older_seq` at or below, `gap` beyond; the first head is `seq` 1 with a zero `prev_hash`); `prev_hash` is the hash of the server's current head (`fork` otherwise); entries are sorted and unique; and **every entry names an item the server holds at that revision with that latest-version hash, state and shredded flag** (`unknown_item`: push the item first). | D54 | 2026-10-17 | **Item revisions are server-assigned, per vault, and sit outside the signature.** `PUT /v1/vaults/{id}/items/{item}` carries `if_match` (the revision the device last saw, zero for new); the server stores the envelope bytes as signed and assigns the next revision of a per-vault counter, or answers `stale` (409) with its current revision and envelope so the device merges and pushes again. | D55 | 2026-10-17 | **Offline heads are provisional and squashed on sync.** Every local mutation still commits a signed head (so the local store stays checkable offline), but heads after the last seq the server confirmed were never on the server: `sync` replaces them with the server's chain, pulls what it lists, and commits **one** new head over the items with their server revisions. Item history is untouched; only the table of contents is rewritten.
